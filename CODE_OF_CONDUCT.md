# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want RVC Dataset Studio to be a welcoming project for everyone who sings, speaks, codes, translates or just asks questions, regardless of age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Many contributors are musicians or language speakers, not programmers — and the reverse.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect people's voices. Never share, request or post recordings of someone without their consent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's voice, accent, body or identity.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information or recordings without explicit permission.
- Using the project, its issues or its community to plan or promote impersonation, fraud or non-consensual voice cloning.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and any official channel — and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in the repository (GitHub profile or security advisory form). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, depending on the severity and repetition of the behaviour.
